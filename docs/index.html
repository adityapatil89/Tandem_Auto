What it does

Most business selling in a dealership gets stuck on the small stuff: keeping a list of fleet buyers, remembering who to call, writing the same message ten times, forgetting to follow up after a test drive. This takes care of that part.

Build your list

Add companies one by one, or import a spreadsheet of company names. Set your area and the kinds of buyer you want.

Scoring

Every company gets a score from 0 to 1, with the reasons listed, so you know who to call first.

First drafts

It starts a message from what you know about the company. Edit it, copy it, and send it from your own email or phone.

Deal tracking

Keep every quote, test drive and follow-up in one list, then export it to your CRM as CSV or JSON.

How it works

Four steps, and you are involved in every one of them.

  1. Set up your area

    Say which cities you cover and which kinds of buyer you want.

  2. Add companies

    Type them in or import a list. The score updates as you add details.

  3. Work the shortlist

    Open the best-scoring companies, add notes, and edit the draft message.

  4. Follow the deal

    Record budget, authority, need, timing, test drives and quotes until the car is delivered, or the deal falls through.

It does the busywork. You make the decisions.

Scores and notes are suggestions built from the details you give it, so check them before you act.

  • It won't send anything. Every message stays a draft until you send it yourself.
  • It shows its reasoning. Each score lists exactly what was added and why.
  • It keeps your data apart. Each account only ever sees its own companies.
  • It stays company-level. Imports ignore columns that look like personal contact details.

Common questions

Things people ask before they sign up.

What does Tandem Auto actually do?

It keeps your business-sales pipeline in one place. You build a list of companies that might buy or recommend cars, it scores them and shows why, you edit a draft message for each, and then you track test drives, quotes and results until the deal is won or lost.

Where do the companies come from?

From you. You add them by hand or import a CSV with company names, plus the website, city and type if you have them. If your administrator has switched on web search, you can also search a city for possible buyers. Those results are only suggestions: you check each one and choose what to add. Any AI-written research notes are labelled so you know to verify them.

How is the score worked out?

Every company starts at 0.15. It gains 0.30 for matching a buyer type you target, 0.25 for being in your area, 0.05 for having a website, and 0.10 if you noted a recent signal. Words in that signal such as fleet, tender or contract add up to 0.15 more. The reasons are listed on each company so nothing is hidden.

Will it message people for me?

No. It has no way to send anything. It prepares a draft, and you copy it into your own email or messaging app when you are happy with it.

Is it for individual car buyers?

Mainly no. It is built around businesses and referral partners, such as fleet operators, corporate buyers, rental and cab companies, insurance agents and finance providers. You would keep handling walk-ins and online enquiries the way you do now.

Is my data private?

Each account sees only its own companies, and passwords are stored hashed, never as plain text. Imports ignore columns that look like personal contact details, such as emails and phone numbers. Check your own legal obligations before you store anything beyond company-level information.

Does it replace my sales team?

No. It handles the lists, scoring and first drafts. Showroom visits, test drives, negotiation and the relationship itself still need a person.

Privacy

We keep your name, work email and a hashed password so you can sign in. We also keep the companies, notes, drafts and deal details you add, and any email you leave to ask for a demo.

We do not ask for personal contact details of individual people, and imports ignore columns that look like them. Please do not enter them in notes.

When web search is switched on, the city and buyer type you search for are sent to a search provider. When AI research is switched on, details of the company you choose are sent to an AI provider. Both are optional and off unless the site owner enables them.

You can export your data at any time. To have your account and its data deleted, email us at the address below.
